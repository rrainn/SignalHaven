# Account access and administration

- Scope: the root account gate, initial-administrator setup, sign-in, account menu, and the administrator-only Users settings section.
- Mode: Operate.
- Audience: self-hosters securing a fresh or upgraded SignalHaven server, plus household users returning to their own guide and recording library.
- Job: establish the first administrator before product setup, authenticate returning users quickly, make the active identity unmistakable, and let an administrator create local username/password accounts.
- Primary actions: Create administrator, Sign in, Create user, and Sign out.
- Important states: checking the server, existing-install upgrade, fresh-install bootstrap, invalid credentials, offline/retry, expired session, username conflict, empty user list, and pending account creation.
- Direction: extend the established Private Broadcast Deck with a focused access panel, restrained brand lockup, visible field labels, one clear action, and compact identity controls in the existing shell.
- Memorable moment: account creation resolves directly into the existing product, with fresh servers continuing into setup and upgraded servers returning to their configured guide without a second decision.
- Constraints: no email, cloud dependency, decorative dashboard, gradients, glass, or duplicated settings chrome; preserve light/dark parity, keyboard access, mobile safe areas, and the existing system-font/tokens/components language.
